Why We Actually Need Diverse Teams

  • Hiring
  • Leadership
  • Teams
Tara Glover September 1, 2023

How often do you look around a meeting room and realize everyone shares the exact same background? What happens when an entire team operates with the exact same blind spots?

Before you actively build a diverse team, you get an echo chamber. You miss critical edge cases. You build products for a narrow slice of the world. After you bring in different voices, you get a team that can actually handle the messy stuff.

Part of being truly transparent means owning up to our knowledge gaps. We all have weaknesses. Embracing diversity drives concrete, real-world benefits for our teams and our projects. Excellent leadership requires connecting with, allying with, and supporting people. It means backing your team completely regardless of their background or appearance.

When we actively value diversity, we naturally attract better projects. We bring in brilliant future candidates. We get noticeably better at building rapport internally and externally. This directly boosts our overall growth.

Cultivating diverse teams across multiple axes helps us understand a much broader range of humans. Look at the concrete results:

  • We build better hypotheses and write cleaner language.
  • We conduct deeper, more accurate research.
  • We ship better software for a much larger audience.
  • We tackle challenges from multiple angles to find highly effective solutions.

Take a hard look at who you are hiring. Look at who you are trusting to solve the weird, wonky bits of your projects. Make a move to bring in different perspectives. See what happens when you let different voices shape the work, and then adjust your hiring pipeline to make it a permanent fix.
