Let's Talk About the Real Value of Going Back to School

  • Higher Education
  • Continuous Learning
Tara Glover June 2, 2026

I’m excited to share that I’ve graduated from Georgetown University School of Continuing Studies with a Master of Professional Studies in Information Technology Management, along with a Graduate Certificate in Lean & Agile Practices.

I’m deeply grateful to the faculty, classmates, colleagues, friends, and family who supported me throughout this journey. Balancing graduate school with my work and familial responsibilities was challenging, but the experience expanded my perspective on strategy, governance, organizational leadership, and the future of technology management. What I value most is not the credential itself, but the opportunity to continue learning, challenge my assumptions, and grow as a leader.

As I look ahead, I’m reminded that the most meaningful part of my career has never been the technology itself—it’s been the people. I genuinely love helping teams move from frustration to fulfillment, rebuilding trust where it has been lost, and creating conditions where people feel excited about their work again. Whether working with engineers, business partners, customers, or executives, there’s nothing more rewarding than seeing people rediscover their confidence, their purpose, and their enthusiasm for what they do.

I’m currently exploring new opportunities where I can bring my experience in technology leadership, program delivery, and organizational transformation to help teams move from frustration to fulfillment. If you know of organizations looking for a technology leader who is passionate about helping people thrive, restoring momentum within teams, and building cultures where both individuals and businesses flourish, I would welcome the opportunity to connect. Hoya Saxa!
