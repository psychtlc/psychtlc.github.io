Consulting & The Delivery Playbook

When projects run late, teams burn out, and margins disappear, you know something is broken. You feel the daily friction.

Most leaders recognize the loud symptoms right away:

  • Projects take way too long.
  • Clients push back on budgets.
  • Teams are drowning in invisible work.
  • Profitability is a total guessing game.

The catch is that those symptoms rarely point to the actual root cause. A margin problem might actually be a sales problem. A communication breakdown is often just a lack of role clarity. We need to figure out what is really going on before we start changing things.

Building Your Delivery Playbook

Before we buy new tools or restructure your teams, we need to understand the reality on the ground. We'll build a customized Delivery Playbook. This is a focused assessment of your actual operations. We'll sit down and map out exactly how work moves through your organization.

Together, we'll examine:

  • How projects are scoped, sold, and planned.
  • The hand-offs between account, project, and production teams.
  • Who actually owns which decisions.
  • The weird, wonky bits slowing your team down.

You'll walk away with a clear picture of your current bottlenecks and a practical, prioritized roadmap for fixing them. I bring an outside perspective and over two decades of experience to untangle the mess. The people closest to the work usually know exactly what is broken. I help you see those patterns and build a system that makes sense.

Putting the Playbook into Action

We'll use your new playbook to make concrete moves. Depending on your needs, we'll focus on a few key areas:

  • Fixing delivery operations: We'll clarify ownership and clear out the daily operational friction.
  • Aligning your teams: We'll build strong, collaborative partnerships between the people managing the clients and the people doing the work.
  • Protecting your margins: We'll improve your estimation and planning practices so you can confidently predict your profitability.

I've spent my career helping teams navigate the messy parts of getting work out the door. You're hiring me to help your humans work better together. We'll implement exactly the right amount of structure and accountability to help your team do its best work. When expectations are clear, the work just gets easier. Teams stop fighting the system and start moving forward.
